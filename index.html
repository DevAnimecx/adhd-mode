FAQ

What is adhd-mode?

adhd-mode ships 10 output rules making AI coding assistants produce ADHD-friendly output. One AGENTS.md file works in 20+ tools including Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Codex, and Claude Code. Rules adapted from i-have-adhd (MIT).

How is adhd-mode different from i-have-adhd?

i-have-adhd is invoked per session. adhd-mode is always on. It also fixes rule decay, fabricated time estimates, and bloat. Covers Copilot, Windsurf, and Gemini that the original doesn't.

How do I install adhd-mode?

Run: curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DevAnimecx/adhd-mode/main/AGENTS.md then restart your editor.

Which tools are supported?

Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Claude Code, Aider, Zed, and 20+ others via AGENTS.md.

Does adhd-mode stop working after a few turns?

No. Anti-decay mechanism with re-injection. Say 'adhd mode' again to re-apply.

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. Works as 'action-first mode' for anyone.

Why no time estimates?

Replaced with scope-by-shape: Quick, Medium, Large.

Is adhd-mode free?

Yes. MIT licensed. No paid features.
